Source: PRD-FUNC-014 (blog), 016 (gallery/results), 017 (contact + inquiry log), 018 (media asset manager); WF-007/008/009/013/023/031/032;

## Caps/roles/menu (MEDIA agent is the ONLY one editing class-admin-roles.php, class-admin-menu.php, admin-core-test.php for all four modules)
New caps: `cc_manage_blog`, `cc_manage_gallery`, `cc_view_inquiries`, `cc_manage_inquiries`, `cc_manage_media`. owner + staff + administrator get all; instructor/student none;
bump the role version. Submenus: `cc-inquiries` (cap cc_view_inquiries → `CC_Admin_Inquiries::render`), `cc-media` (cap cc_manage_media → `CC_Admin_Media::render`).
Blog, Gallery and Results use their own native CPT admin menus gated by the CPT capabilities mapped to the caps above (see below).
